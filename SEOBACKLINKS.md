## Priority Order (Effort vs Impact)
### Tier 1 (Do first — highest ROI)
1. Google Business Profile — verify + post frequency
2. Product Hunt — plan a launch campaign
3. Crunchbase — create company profile
4. G2 / Capterra / GetApp — claim + encourage reviews
5. LinkedIn Company Page — optimize with keywords + posts

### Tier 2 (Strong backlinks, moderate effort)
6. BetaList / BetaPage — submit with description
7. SaaS Hub / SoftwareSuggest / SaaSWorthy
8. IndiaMart / Justdial / TradeIndia — list as logistics service
9. Futurepedia / Toolify / There's An AI For That — AI tag
10. AlternativeTo — list as alternative to traditional brokers

### Tier 3 (Lower effort, fill gaps)
11. Remaining India directories (Sulekha, Click.in, Hotfrog)
12. Remaining logistics directories
13. Remaining AI directories
14. General directories (Manta, MerchantCircle, Cylex)

## Submission Tips
- Use consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) everywhere — this builds local SEO
- Write unique descriptions per directory — don't copy-paste
- Use "LoadSaathi" as the business name consistently
- Category: Freight Services / Logistics / Transport / Trucking
- Keywords: truck booking, freight transport, logistics platform India, load matching
- Add logo + images where allowed (improves conversion)
